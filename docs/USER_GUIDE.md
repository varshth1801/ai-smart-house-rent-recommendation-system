## Key Features & How to Use Them

### 1. AI Recommendation Engine
1. Navigate to **AI Match** or the home page.
2. Adjust your target monthly budget, preferred city, bedroom count, and lifestyle (e.g. *Tech Worker*, *Quiet*, *Family*, *Pet Lover*).
3. The system ranks properties with visual match percentage meters (e.g. 96% Match) and explains why each property fits your preferences.

### 2. Natural Language Property Search
1. In the search bar on the Explore page, type conversational queries such as:
   - *"Quiet 2bhk near tech park under 35000 with balcony"*
   - *"Luxury sea view 3 bedroom with pool"*
   - *"Affordable 1bhk for student under 25k"*
2. The AI parser extracts bedrooms, price ceilings, and amenities automatically.

### 3. Rental Price Predictor
1. Click **AI Price Predictor** in the navigation bar.
2. Enter the property specs (Square Footage, Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Location Tier, Amenities).
3. View the AI estimated monthly fair market rent, confidence intervals, price per square foot, and pricing factors.

### 4. Side-by-Side Property Comparison
1. Select **Compare Homes** from the navigation bar.
2. Select 2 or 3 properties to compare specs, amenities, pros/cons, and see which home the AI selects as the value winner.

### 5. AI Virtual Real Estate Assistant (Chatbot)
1. Click the floating AI Chat icon in the bottom right corner of the screen.
2. Ask questions about deposit policies, pet rules, neighborhood vibes, or request recommendations.

### 6. Real-Time WebSocket Notifications
- When a tenant applies for a rental or books a tour, the landlord immediately receives an in-app notification without refreshing.
- When a landlord updates the application status, the tenant receives a real-time notification alert.
